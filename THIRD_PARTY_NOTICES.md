# Third-party data notices

The Apache-2.0 license in this repository applies to the software, not to
third-party source data or derivatives.

Every source and document record preserves its own `license_id`, license/terms
URL, reviewed date, attribution, and release permissions. Public packages are
partitioned under `license_shards/`. CC BY-SA material remains in a compatible
share-alike shard. Manifest-only and blocked sources are represented only by
the scrubbed reconstruction manifest; their binaries, canonical text,
passages, and QA are not included.

Wikipedia attribution must preserve article title, revision ID, URL, and
history/contributor access. Wikidata profiles are CC0. OurAirports-derived
codes must not be described as officially assigned by IATA or ICAO unless a
separate authoritative citation supports that individual claim.
