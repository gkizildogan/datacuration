# Language ratio observations

Language balance is reported as an observation rather than used to reject corpus
documents. The 70% English / 30% Turkish corpus reference has a ±5 percentage-point
reporting tolerance. QA planning remains 50% English / 50% Turkish, also with a
±5-point reporting tolerance for accepted QA.

The measurements recorded before this change were:

- Archived promoted QA: 70.4% English and 29.6% Turkish.
- Active v1.1 accepted QA: approximately 50% English and 50% Turkish.
- Curated corpus tokens: 75.0% English and 25.0% Turkish.

The bounded Wikipedia development collection already reaches its configured
400-English and 600-Turkish page caps. The expanded aviation categories remain in
the source registry; later scaling should increase those caps instead of adding more
topic roots.
